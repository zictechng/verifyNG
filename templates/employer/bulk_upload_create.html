How it works

  1. Download the CSV template and fill in your candidates.
  2. Each row needs at least a full_name and either a phone_number or NIN.
  3. Upload the completed file below — maximum 500 rows and 5 MB.
  4. VerifyNG will process each candidate and show results on the upload detail page.
{# CSV columns reference #}

CSV columns

Column Required? Notes
full_name Required Candidate's full legal name
phone_number One required Nigerian mobile number, e.g. 08012345678
nin One required 11-digit NIN — hashed immediately, never stored as plain text

Phone or NIN — at least one must be provided per row. Rows with neither will be skipped.
