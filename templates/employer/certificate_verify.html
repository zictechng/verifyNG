How does TrustPass verification work?
Workforce Clearance Registry Guidelines:
  • 3-Point Identity Matching: Certificate Reference ID, Candidate Surname, and Date of Birth must match the national registry record.
  • 30-Day Enrollment Window: Freshly issued clearance certificates are live-searchable for 30 calendar days post-issuance.
  • Continuous Verification: Beyond 30 days, certificates must be enrolled in continuous monitoring Services to remain current and verifiable online. Certificates not enrolled within this window cannot be searched and require a new application.
