Data Protection & Privacy Policy

1. Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)

{{ site_config.site_name|default:"VerifyNG" }} processes personal data in strict compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and NDPR guidelines. We respect individual consent and privacy rights at every tier of our platform.

2. Cryptographic One-Way Hashing

National Identification Numbers (NIN), Bank Verification Numbers (BVN), and sensitive identifiers submitted for background checks or identity verification are converted to SHA-256 cryptographic hashes immediately upon receipt. Raw sensitive government numbers are never stored in plaintext within our databases.

3. Real-Time "Who Checked Me" Transparency

Individuals have complete visibility into their data. When an employer, agency, or institution runs a background check against your credentials, the inquiry is recorded in your personal log so you can review who checked you and when.

4. Right of Access and Account Deletion

You retain the right to download your activity logs, update your contact details, or request permanent deletion of your profile via the Account Settings menu.

Terms of Service & Legal Framework

1. Permitted Platform Use

{{ site_config.site_name|default:"VerifyNG" }} provides fraud reporting, candidate background verification, and identity credentialing. Access is granted solely for legitimate employment screening, anti-fraud prevention, and commercial due diligence.

2. Good-Faith Reporting & Anti-Defamation

All fraud reports and dispute filings must be submitted in good faith with verifiable evidence. Submitting deliberately fabricated, malicious, or defamatory reports violates our Terms of Service and will result in immediate permanent account termination and referral to regulatory authorities.

3. Employer & Agency Obligations

Employers, recruitment firms, and authorized security agencies screening candidates agree to obtain prospective employee consent in accordance with local employment laws before initiating checks.

4. Contact & Legal Notices

For legal notices or data inquiries, please write to our compliance office at support@verifyng.com.
