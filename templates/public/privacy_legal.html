1

Categories of Data We Collect & Process

To deliver verification services, prevent crime, and operate our community fraud defense platform, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Account & Registration Information: Full legal name, official business entity name, verified email address, telephone number, role classification (Individual, SME, Corporate Employer, Agency), and cryptographic password hash.
  • Cryptographic Identity Tokens: Bank Account Numbers, Bank Verification Numbers (BVN), and National Identification Numbers (NIN) submitted for authentication. (See Section 2 regarding our zero-plaintext cryptographic standard.)
  • Fraud Allegation & Evidentiary Submissions: Suspect names, reported phone numbers, social media handles, website URLs, transaction dates, monetary loss figures, and documentary exhibits (transfer receipts, invoices, chat screenshots, contracts).
  • Automated Telemetry & Device Metadata: IP addresses, browser fingerprint, device operating system, referring URL, time-stamps, geolocation indicators, and session tokens to combat bot traffic and detect fraudulent report stuffing.
2

One-Way Cryptographic Hashing Standard (NIN, BVN & Bank Accounts)

We implement strict zero-knowledge cryptographic safeguards for sensitive government and financial identifiers:

Irreversible Peppered SHA-256 Hashing: National Identification Numbers (NIN) and Bank Verification Numbers (BVN) submitted for verification queries are immediately converted into irreversible SHA-256 cryptographic hashes with a platform-side secret pepper.

No Plaintext Storage: Raw, plaintext NIN or BVN digits are never written to permanent disk storage, database tables, or application logs. Matches are computed strictly via mathematical hash comparisons. Even in the event of an unauthorized physical database extraction, reverse-engineering raw government identifiers from our records is cryptographically impossible.
