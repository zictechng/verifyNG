Consumer Defense Shield

5 Golden Rules to Protect Yourself Online

Follow these battle-tested security practices before making any online purchase, hiring a contractor, or transferring funds.

1
Run a 10-Second Pre-Check
Critical

Before sending money to any individual or business, copy their 10-digit bank account number and phone number and paste them into VerifyNG Quick Check. You will immediately see if other victims have flagged the account for fraud.

2
Beware of Mismatched Bank Names
Watch Out

If an online store calls itself "Lagos Luxury Hub" but asks you to pay into a personal savings account under an unrelated individual's name with excuses like "that's our accountant's account," stop immediately. Demand a verified corporate account or a registered business entity.

3
Reject Manufactured Urgency
Psychology

Scammers use psychological pressure: "Pay in 15 minutes or it sells to someone else," or "Clearance price valid for today only." Legitimate, honest vendors respect your right to verify their credentials and do not rush financial decisions.

4
Demand Video Calls or Physical Pickup
Verification

For high-value items (smartphones, electronics, vehicles, rental apartments), request a live video call showing the merchandise with today's date written on paper, or opt for payment on delivery via a reputable escrow or verified logistics agent.

Golden Rule #5: Never Share OTPs, BVN, or Card PINs

No official from {{ site_config.site_name|default:"VerifyNG" }}, your commercial bank, or any government agency will ever call, text, or WhatsApp you requesting your 4-digit card PIN, banking password, or 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP). Anyone asking for these is an active fraudster.

Emergency Fraud Protocol

Just Sent Money to a Scammer? Act Now!

Time is critical. Follow these four urgent steps within the first 1 to 24 hours to maximize your chances of freezing funds and recovering your money.

Step 1 • Immediate
Call Your Bank's Fraud Desk

Call your bank's emergency customer care line immediately. State that you made a fraudulent transfer, provide the session ID and the recipient's bank details, and request an urgent inter-bank recall (NIP recall).

Step 2 • Within 1 Hour
Contact Recipient's Bank

Reach out to the fraud department of the bank that received your money. Notify them that the beneficiary account has been used to perpetrate fraud so they can place a Post-No-Debit (PND) restriction.

Step 3 • File on VerifyNG
Submit Incident Report

File an incident report on VerifyNG. This alerts our national moderation desk, flags the account across our platform, and shields others immediately.

Step 4 • Official Report
Report to Police / EFCC

Visit the nearest Police station (Cybercrime division) or EFCC office to lodge a formal petition with your bank statements, chats, and VerifyNG case reference to secure a court order for refund.
